GTA6 Clipper

A private publishing tool for GTA 6 highlight clip channels.

GTA6 Clipper is an internal tool used by a single operator to publish short highlight clips from Grand Theft Auto VI livestreams to the operator's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. Clips come only from streamers whose clipping programmes the operator has joined, and every clip credits the streamer.

What it does

  • Cuts short vertical clips from livestreams the operator is permitted to clip.
  • Adds captions and a title, and uploads the clip to the operator's own channels through the official YouTube and Instagram APIs.
  • Reads view counts of those uploads to track performance.

Who can use it

Only its operator. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in or collect data from other people.
